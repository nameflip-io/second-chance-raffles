    FAQ

    Can't find what you need? Our how it works page and full terms cover the detail.

    How do the draws work?

    Every competition has a fixed number of tickets and a closing date. When the countdown hits zero, entries close and we draw the winner live on Instagram. The draw happens on the advertised date even if not every ticket has sold, so there is always a winner.

    What are my odds?

    Your odds are printed on every competition. Entries are capped, so one ticket in a 1,999-ticket draw is a 1 in 1,999 chance. Ten tickets is 10 in 1,999. If fewer tickets sell, your real odds are better than the number shown, never worse.

    Is there a free way to enter?

    Yes. Every competition can be entered free by post, and postal entries go into the same draw with exactly the same odds as paid ones. Full instructions, including the address and what to include, are on our free postal entry page.

    What is the Second Chance draw?

    It's our promise that a losing ticket isn't the end. Every non-winning entry from that month's competitions goes into a Second Chance draw at month end, automatically and at no extra cost. It's drawn live, just like everything else.

    How are winners picked?

    Each entry gets a ticket number. On draw night we use an independent random number generator, live on camera, to pick the winning number. Only entries with the correct answer to the competition question are eligible. Every draw is recorded and the winning ticket number is posted on our winners page.

    When do I get my prize?

    We contact winners by phone and email within 24 hours of the draw. Cash prizes are paid by bank transfer within 48 hours of us confirming your details. Physical prizes are usually delivered within 14 days, and we arrange car handovers with you directly. Where a cash alternative is offered, the choice is yours.
