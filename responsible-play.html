Keep it fun

  • Only spend what you can afford to lose. Treat tickets as entertainment, not a way to make money.
  • Decide on a budget before you play, and stick to it.
  • Never chase losses by buying more tickets.
  • Take regular breaks, and don't play when you're stressed, upset or have been drinking.

Warning signs

It may be time to take a break if you:

  • spend more than you planned, or more than you can afford;
  • borrow money or miss bills to buy tickets;
  • feel anxious, guilty or irritable about how much you spend;
  • hide your spending from friends or family.
