Free postal entry

Every competition can be entered for free by post. A postal entry goes into the same draw, with exactly the same odds, as a paid ticket. Here’s how.

What to write

On a postcard, or a sheet of paper in an envelope, write clearly in capitals:

  1. Your full name
  2. Your full postal address, including postcode
  3. Your phone number
  4. Your email address
  5. The competition name, exactly as shown on the site
  6. Your answer to that competition’s question

Missing or unreadable details mean we can’t enter you, so double-check before you post.

Keep it valid

  • One entry per envelope or postcard. Several entries in one envelope count as a single entry.
  • First- or second-class post. We can’t accept hand-delivered, recorded or franked bulk entries.
  • Arrive before entries close. Your entry must reach us before the closing time shown on the competition. Late entries aren’t entered.
  • Same limit as paid entries. Postal and paid entries count towards the same per-person limit for each competition.
  • Confirmation. We’ll email you when your entry is added, usually within [X] working days of it arriving.

Same odds.

A postal entry is treated exactly like a paid ticket. It gets a ticket number, it goes into the same live draw, and if it doesn’t win, it goes into the monthly Second Chance draw too.
