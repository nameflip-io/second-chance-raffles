How it works

Pick a prize. Grab your tickets. Answer one question. Watch the live draw. Didn’t win? Your ticket rolls into the monthly Second Chance draw. Here’s every step, in plain English.

From ticket to winner

  1. Pick your prize

    Browse the live competitions: cars, tech, tax-free cash and luxury. Every listing shows the ticket price, how many tickets exist in total, how many have sold, and exactly when entries close.

  2. Choose your tickets

    Pick a bundle or slide to the number you want, up to the per-person limit shown. Each ticket gets its own number. The more tickets you hold, the better your odds, and the page shows those odds as you choose.

  3. Answer the question

    Every competition has a simple skill question with three options. It’s what makes this a prize competition, not a lottery. Only entries with the correct answer go into the draw.

  4. Watch the live draw

    When the countdown ends, we draw the winner live on Instagram, usually on a Friday at 8pm. The draw goes ahead on the date shown even if the competition hasn’t sold out, so there is always a winner.

  5. Claim your prize

    We call and email the winner within 24 hours. Cash is paid within 48 hours of confirming your details, and physical prizes are usually delivered within 14 days. Where there’s a cash alternative, it’s your call.

  6. Didn’t win? You’re still in.

    Every non-winning ticket goes into the monthly Second Chance draw. No extra cost and nothing to do.

How winners are picked

No back rooms, no hand-picked names. Here’s exactly what happens on draw night.

  1. Entries close. At the advertised time, or earlier if every ticket sells. Nobody can buy a ticket after that.
  2. Correct answers only. We remove any entry with the wrong answer to the question. Postal entries are added on the same terms.
  3. Random number generator. On camera, we use an independent random number generator to pick one ticket number from all valid entries.
  4. Streamed live. The whole draw goes out live on Instagram, and the recording stays on our profile.
  5. Published. The winning ticket number and the winner’s first name and town go on our winners page, with their permission.

Odds, explained

Your odds depend on two things: how many tickets exist, and how many you hold. Because every competition is capped, the worst case is printed on the ticket.

FAQ

Still stuck? The full rules are in our terms & conditions.

How do the draws work?

Every competition has a fixed number of tickets and a closing date. When the countdown hits zero, or every ticket sells, entries close and we draw the winner live on Instagram. The draw happens on the advertised date even if not every ticket has sold, so there is always a winner.

What are my odds?

Your odds are printed on every competition. Entries are capped, so one ticket in a 1,999-ticket draw is a 1 in 1,999 chance. Ten tickets is 10 in 1,999. If fewer tickets sell, your real odds are better than the number shown, never worse.

Is there a free way to enter?

Yes. Every competition can be entered free by post, and postal entries go into the same draw with exactly the same odds as paid ones. Full instructions are on our free postal entry page.

What is the Second Chance draw?

It’s our promise that a losing ticket isn’t the end. Every non-winning entry from that month’s competitions, paid or postal, goes into a Second Chance draw at month end, automatically and at no extra cost. It’s drawn live, just like everything else.

How are winners picked?

Each entry gets a ticket number. On draw night we use an independent random number generator, live on camera, to pick the winning number. Only entries with the correct answer are eligible. Every draw is recorded and the winning ticket number posted on our winners page.

When do I get my prize?

We contact winners by phone and email within 24 hours of the draw. Cash prizes are paid by bank transfer within 48 hours of us confirming your details. Physical prizes are usually delivered within 14 days, and we arrange car handovers with you directly.

Why is there a question?

UK law treats a draw you pay to enter as a lottery unless there’s a genuine test of skill or a free entry route. We have both. Only entries with the correct answer go into the draw, so take a second to get it right.

What if I answer the question wrong?

Entries with a wrong answer aren’t entered into the draw. You can change your answer any time before you pay, so take a second to get it right. Our terms cover the detail.

How many tickets can I buy?

Each competition shows its own limit, usually between 50 and 100 tickets per person. Paid and postal entries count towards the same limit.

Can I get a refund?

Once a ticket is bought it can’t be refunded, unless we cancel a competition. If that ever happens, every paid entrant is refunded in full. See our terms for the detail.

Who can enter?

You must be 18 or over and live in the United Kingdom. Our staff and their immediate families can’t enter.

Do I pay tax on my prize?

Prizes from competitions like ours are generally not taxed as income in the UK. This isn’t tax advice, so speak to an adviser if you’re unsure about your own situation.
